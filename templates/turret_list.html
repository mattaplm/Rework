Scrap write-off, by week

Scrap recorded in one week is written off in SyteLine the following week (ERP uses that later week's date) -- e.g. scrap recorded in week 42 gets written off in week 43. Each row groups together every scrap recording for that item due in that write-off week, which can come from several different jobs -- "Production week(s)" shows every distinct production week those jobs came from, for context (the write-off week itself is derived from the date the scrap was actually recorded, not a job's production week), and "Lines" is how many separate scrap recordings got added together into that row's "Qty scrapped" total.
